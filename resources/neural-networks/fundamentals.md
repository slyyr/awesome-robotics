### 3Blue1Brown: Neural Networks

**Type:** Video series

**Access:** Free

**Level:** Beginner

**Best for:** Absolute beginners and visual learners who want the core ideas before implementing them.

**Prerequisites:** High-school math; basic calculus and linear algebra help for the backpropagation chapters.
