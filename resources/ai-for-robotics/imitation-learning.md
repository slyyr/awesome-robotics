## Resources

### Diffusion Policy: Visuomotor Policy Learning via Action Diffusion

**Type:** Research paper

**Access:** Open access

**Level:** Advanced

**Format:** Paper (arXiv) with project page and code

Introduces robot visuomotor policies represented as a conditional denoising diffusion process over actions. The paper explains why this handles multimodal action distributions well and how receding-horizon control and visual conditioning make it work on physical robots.

**Best for:** Readers who want to understand a leading imitation-learning method for robot manipulation.

**Prerequisites:** Deep learning, basic imitation learning; familiarity with diffusion models helps.

[Open resource →](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.04137)
