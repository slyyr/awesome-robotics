### Learn PyTorch for Deep Learning (learnpytorch.io)

**Type:** Online book / tutorial

**Access:** Free

**Level:** Beginner → Intermediate

**Format:** Online book + notebooks + exercises

Code-first introduction to PyTorch: tensors, the training workflow, classification, computer vision, custom datasets, transfer learning and paper replication. Only the online book is free; the companion video course is a paid product and is not linked here.

**Best for:** Programmers who are new to PyTorch and want to learn by writing code.

**Prerequisites:** Basic Python; no prior deep learning experience required.
