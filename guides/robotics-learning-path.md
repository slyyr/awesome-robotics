# Robotics Learning Path

[← Guides](README.md)

![Robotics pipeline](../assets/diagrams/robotics-pipeline.svg)

1. **Prerequisites.** [Linear algebra](../resources/math/linear-algebra.md), [calculus](../resources/math/calculus.md), [classical mechanics](../resources/physics/classical-mechanics.md), [Python](../resources/programming/python.md) and [C++](../resources/programming/cpp.md).
2. **Big picture.** [Robotics fundamentals](../resources/robotics/robotics-fundamentals.md).
3. **Modeling and control.** [Kinematics & dynamics](../resources/robotics/kinematics-dynamics.md), then [control](../resources/robotics/control.md).
4. **Sensing and estimation.** [Sensors & fusion](../resources/robotics/sensors-sensor-fusion.md), [perception](../resources/robotics/perception.md), [SLAM](../resources/robotics/localization-mapping-slam.md).
5. **Acting.** [Planning & navigation](../resources/robotics/planning-navigation.md), [manipulation](../resources/robotics/manipulation.md).
6. **Tooling.** [ROS 2](../resources/robotics/ros-2.md) and [simulation](../resources/simulation/README.md), used from step 3 onward.
7. **Frontier.** [Robot learning](../resources/robotics/robot-learning.md), [embodied AI](../resources/robotics/embodied-ai.md).
