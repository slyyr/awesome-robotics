# Physics Prerequisites

[← Guides](README.md)

You do not need a physics degree. For robotics, prioritize:

1. [Classical mechanics](../resources/physics/classical-mechanics.md): forces, energy, rotation.
2. [Electricity & electronics](../resources/physics/electricity-electronics.md): circuits, sensors, motors.
3. [Physics for robotics](../resources/physics/physics-for-robotics.md): friction, contact, actuators.

For software-only AI work, physics is optional.
