# How to Build an AI / Robotics Portfolio

[← Guides](README.md)

- **Show, don't list.** A short video or GIF of the robot or model working beats a long README.
- **Document decisions.** What you tried, what failed, why you changed course.
- **Cover the stack.** One perception or ML project, one planning/control project, one integrated system.
- **Make it reproducible.** Clear setup instructions, pinned versions, a simulation demo.
- **Evaluate honestly.** Report metrics and failure cases.

See [Projects](../resources/projects/README.md) for what to build.
