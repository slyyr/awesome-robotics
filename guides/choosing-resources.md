# How to Choose Courses, Books & Resources

[← Guides](README.md)

1. **Pick the format that matches your goal.** Books for depth, courses for structure, lecture notes for reference, projects for skill.
2. **Check prerequisites honestly.** Start one level lower if unsure.
3. **Sample before committing.** Try one lecture or chapter.
4. **Do the exercises.** Passive watching teaches little.
5. **Limit yourself to one main resource per subject.** Others are supplements.
6. **Check access labels.** *Free*, *Free to audit*, *Open access* and *Free tier* mean different things.
